## Critical Scientific and Legal Boundaries (Cautions)
1. **Legal Immunity:** Software cannot confer legal immunity. System shows evidence present/missing; worded honestly.
2. **Pathogen-Free Flag:** Single probe does not cover pile and re-contamination is possible. Result is "Criteria met", "Criteria not met", or "Insufficient evidence".
3. **Kinetic Parameters:** D-value targets and log-reduction parameters are configurable and cite sources under `[EXPERT-REVIEW]`.
4. **65°C Upper Boundary:** Fire risk depends on dryness, pile size, and duration; upper thresholds configurable.
5. **Reduced Disturbance in Curing:** Turning reduced in frequency with human override for compaction/odor.
6. **Methane Thresholds:** Low-cost MQ-4 sensors provide indicator trends, not calibrated laboratory ppm.
7. **Fixed Recipe Doses:** Lime and additives scaled dynamically to mass from reviewed library.
8. **Product Standards:** FCO schedules depend on category; tagged `[LEGAL-REVIEW]`.
9. **ROI Formula:** Savings computed from stored data with visible assumptions.
10. **Camera Darkness Index:** Calibrated against reference patch and moisture; not standalone maturity.
