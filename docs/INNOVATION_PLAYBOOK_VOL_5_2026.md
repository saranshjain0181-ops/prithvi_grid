# Prithvi-Grid Innovation Playbook, Volume 5: Shared Services, Craft Operations and Leadership Dashboards
Prepared for the Prithvi-Grid founding team · 25 October 2026 · Volume 5: twenty-four new features that make the whole library govern, connect and scale

## Purpose and scope of this volume
The master prompt and the first four volumes of this playbook describe close to a hundred features. Volume 1 built around the Smart Spear, the evidence it produces and the growth of the business. Volume 2 covered the craft of making compost and the management pages around it. Volume 3 added the compatibility backbone (the Event Spine, the Orchestrator, the Entity Graph and the Registry), more craft, and the first set of role dashboards. Volume 4 added the bioprocess intelligence core, with the Phase Engine, and six more dashboards. This fifth volume does not repeat any of them. Where it builds on an earlier feature, it names it as the master prompt or by volume and feature number, so your agent can reuse what exists.

At this size, the main risk to the product is no longer a missing feature. It is that cross-cutting concerns which every feature touches have been left implicit: who is allowed to approve what, how photos and voice notes are kept and deleted, how outside systems connect, how a field actuator is switched safely, how printed signs and labels are produced, and how the phones and tablets that run the apps are kept healthy. Part A of this volume therefore proposes six shared services that every other feature must use instead of reinventing. Part B proposes twelve new features for the craft and operations of compost and manure making that the earlier volumes did not cover: deciding which waste goes down which pathway, preventing waste at the source, auditing waste composition, keeping the yard hygienic, managing odor at the fence line and the relationship with neighbours, advising buyers how to use compost, proving performance in the field, handling product complaints and recalls, training workers on a practice pit, running a shift huddle, running a regular review rhythm and learning from hardware failures. Part C proposes six more dashboards for leadership questions the earlier dashboards do not answer: integrated operations planning, yard performance, client finance and procurement, workforce, stakeholder experience, and growth and expansion.

## Critical Scientific, Privacy and Legal Boundaries (Cautions)
1. **Actuator Safety [EXPERT-REVIEW]:** Physical relays, blowers, and valves start in Advisory mode; assisted/automatic requires certified electrical safety checks and hardware watchdog fail-safes.
2. **External Processor Transfer & Licences [LEGAL-REVIEW]:** Waste consignments, external disposal manifests, and mass balance diversion records; strictly excludes biomedical waste.
3. **Product Complaint & Recall Liability [LEGAL-REVIEW]:** Consumer liability wording, mandatory two-approver sign-off for recalls, quarantine containment, and retained sample verification.
4. **Media Retention, Consent & Redaction [LEGAL-REVIEW]:** Automated deterministic face/voice blurring before external sharing, pseudonymisation preserving cryptographic audit seals.
5. **Compost Agronomic Advice & Performance Claims [EXPERT-REVIEW] & [LEGAL-REVIEW]:** Guidance based strictly on certified lab batches and approved crop profiles; all claims pass the Claims Gate.
6. **Configurable Emission & Regulatory Standards:** Configurable parameters with source citations; never hardcoded constants.
7. **Hindi Vernacular Language [NEEDS-NATIVE-REVIEW]:** Respectful terminology (Safai Mitra, Palatna, Abhyas Pit, Aaj Ka Plan).
